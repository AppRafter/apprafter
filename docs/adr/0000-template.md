# ADR NNNN: Title of the decision

## Status

`Proposed` | `Accepted` | `Deprecated` | `Superseded by ADR NNNN`

Date: YYYY-MM-DD.

## Context

What is the issue we are facing? What forces are at play (technical,
operational, organisational, legal)? Describe the background a future
reader needs in order to evaluate whether the decision still applies.
Use neutral language; this is not the place to advocate.

## Decision

The change we are proposing or have agreed to. State it as a clear
imperative: "We will use X instead of Y." Be specific: name versions,
boundaries, and what is in / out of scope.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder after this decision is applied? List
both positive and negative effects, plus neutral ones worth recording.

## Alternatives considered

What other options were on the table? Why were they rejected? A
future reader should not have to re-derive this list.

## Risks

Concrete failure modes or unknowns introduced by this decision. Each
risk should have a mitigation note or an explicit "we accept this".

## Owner

Person or team responsible for the ADR: convening reviews, approving
amendments, sunsetting.

## Re-evaluation

Trigger that should cause this decision to be revisited:

- a date ("revisit after 2026-12-01"),
- a milestone ("at platform M5"),
- or a measurable signal ("if reconcile latency exceeds 200 ms p99").

If none, write "no scheduled re-evaluation".

## References

Links to specs, prior art, related ADRs, blog posts, source code.
