# Code of Conduct

The AppRafter project adopts the **Contributor Covenant Code of
Conduct, version 2.1**, in full. The canonical text is at:

<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

## Summary

We pledge to make participation in the AppRafter community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at
**conduct@apprafter.dev** (placeholder until a real address is
provisioned). Reports are reviewed promptly, investigated fairly,
and treated as confidential.

## Enforcement

The Enforcement Guidelines from Contributor Covenant 2.1 are used
verbatim — Correction → Warning → Temporary Ban → Permanent Ban.
Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the
standards and may take any action they deem appropriate, up to and
including a permanent ban.
